## Void weapon models

The Void rarity knives and snipers are real 3D models from [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com),
used under their Creative Commons Attribution licences. VoidFlow fits each one into the hand
(scaled, turned, display stands and extra pieces left out); textures are downscaled for the web
build. In game, each Void item's card names its model's author.
