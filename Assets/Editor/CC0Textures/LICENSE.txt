Textures in this folder are from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), released under the
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal license (public domain): free to use, modify and publish,
commercially too, with no attribution required. Credit is given here anyway.

Leather034C (stitched quilted leather), Leather026 (smooth black leather), Leather018
(perforated leather), Fabric082A (fine woven fabric): color, normal (OpenGL) and roughness
maps, resized to 512x512. Editor-only sources: GrayboxBuilder.Gloves bakes the glove and
jacket textures from them (Resources/Arms); these files are not shipped in builds.
